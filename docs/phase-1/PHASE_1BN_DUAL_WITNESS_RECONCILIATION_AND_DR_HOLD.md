## Threat model and source-of-truth boundary

Phase 1BM persists a locked, MySQL-only candidate quote checkpoint head and accepted/incident attempts. It cannot independently prove that its witness head was ever published outside the application or that its candidate key/revocation source belongs to a legally authorized organization. Phase 1BN adds strictly bounded **two-source candidate observations** and a second, migration-checksummed *local* reconciliation history. No downloaded third-party head, independent CA key-enrollment record, authenticated CRL/OCSP, cloud Object Lock retention receipt or provider DR witness is available. Quorum **of two self-described sources** is not actual independence, even when both Ed25519 signatures verify.

## Implementation

1. `server/commercialIntegrity/quoteHeadReconciliation.ts` provides pure offline `assessCandidateQuoteHeadReconciliation`. It checks **two distinct claimed source IDs signed by distinct candidate Ed25519 public keys**, strict no-extra-fields body, quote ID, domain-separated canonical signatures, pinned-key validity and revoked-key denial; compares source digest, BM candidate receipt SHA and sequence to the locked local head. Published head must not be older than 30 minutes, claimed observation older than 15 minutes, or more than 1 minute in the future relative to explicitly supplied `now`. Revocation `thisUpdate` must be at most 60 minutes old, `nextUpdate` future, claim epoch positive; the two candidate sources must agree on epoch, revocation timestamps and sorted revoked key IDs. Disagreement, split heads, local lag, rollback, replayed stale data, shadow fields, unpinned or expired keys all cause explicit anomaly codes and HARD HOLD.
2. `canonicalCandidateQuoteRollover` tests *both* old and new pinned-key Ed25519 signatures over an exact candidate key-rollover statement, with unique key IDs, matching quote and source receipt, effective time and rationale. A cryptographically valid fixture only says the packet is internally consistent: **it does not authorize rotation or independent key enrollment**. Any changed local custody pinset triggers permanent candidate quarantine pending authenticated external change approval.
3. DR rehearsal input `recoverySnapshot` is compared with held BM source/receipt/sequence; a lower restored sequence raises `BN_DR_REHEARSAL_ROLLBACK_DETECTED`, a same-sequence fork or later inconsistency raises `BN_DR_REHEARSAL_HEAD_MISMATCH`. This never claims a real offsite encrypted backup, independently witnessed restore, WORM retention, availability, RTO or RPO.

Staging requires independent `BN_RECONCILIATION_WRITE_APPROVED=YES` *in addition* to prior `BM_QUOTE_WRITE_APPROVED=YES`, `STAGING_DISPOSABLE_CONFIRMED=YES`, configured MySQL TLS and a disposable `aaditech_staging_*` database. ## Security limits — never call this a verified independent trust chain

All input observations, keys, CRL/OCSP timestamps, revocation epochs and claimed publication metadata in Phase 1BN are supplied by internal/synthetic callers. Two different self-declared sources with separate Ed25519 signing keys are **not** independently authenticated. A single malicious database operator can still rewrite both original quote history and the entire local BM/BN ledger. There is no authenticated independently retained monotonic witness head, no real production-approved independent witness provider, no legal MCA/GST company/signatory verification, no genuine supplier quote or vendor identity confirmation, no CCA/eMudhra live CRL/OCSP, no cloud WORM denied deletion, no third-party encrypted DR restoration, and no signed OWNER/LEGAL/SECURITY/OPERATIONS release signoff.

Independent anti-rollback assurance requires external, legally approved, separately operated and authenticated publisher keys, witnessed monotonic head publication retained **outside** this repository/database, independent certificate/revocation validation and actual WORM/DR recovery proofs. A successful synthetic test is not a signoff.
