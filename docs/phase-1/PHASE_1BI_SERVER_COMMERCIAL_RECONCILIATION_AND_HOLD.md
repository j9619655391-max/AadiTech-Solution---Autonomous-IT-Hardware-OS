**Date:** 2026-11-17 IST. **Correct repository:** `j9619655391-max/AadiTech-Solution---Autonomous-IT-Hardware-OS`, branch `chore/phase0-foundation-20261106`.
**Status:** Internal engineering only; test evidence is accepted only when latest GitHub HEAD's baseline and disposable-MySQL jobs both pass. **No production release, no real provider authentication, and no external supplier or customer dispatch.**

## What existed already (preserved, not reimplemented)

- `server/quotes/router.ts` maintains owner-scoped, MySQL-transactional, parameterized **DRAFT** quotation creation from server-held CRM customer/catalog records. Request UUID idempotency and payload digests prevent duplicate or conflicting retry inserts; original per-line prices/costs/GST/HSN are snapshotted, and revisions are monotonic. Quote math uses integer paise. These CRM records are **not externally verified real company identities**, nor independently authenticated vendor pricing.
- `server/quotes/reviewRouter.ts` maintains separately authenticated `REVIEWER` internal margin/revision decisions, explicit below-policy override justification, and one decision per revision. No official quote, e-invoice or customer consent/acceptance exists.
- `server/quotes/releaseHoldRouter.ts` creates a held quote snapshot, with `HELD` preparation and `DISABLED` outbox, without delivery worker.
- `server/procurement/ownerRouter.ts` and `reviewRouter.ts` maintain owner-scoped supplier and purchase-request drafts, independent internal supplier/requisition review, UUID idempotency, step-up and MFA, and `HELD` `DISABLED` PO snapshot. The client-supplied supplier quote **evidence reference** is only an applicant assertion; its content/issuer is not authenticated. There is **no** actual issued supplier PO, GRN, posted stock, bank settlement or approved external endpoint.

## Improvements shipped in this phase

1. **Session-bound password step-up at commercial review and release.** Quote-review `POST /api/quotes/reviews/:id` and quote-release preparation `POST /api/quotes/release-preparations/:id` now additionally require `requireStepUp`, after role check and `requirePrivilegedMfa`. Step-up is bound to the exact session cookie and expires in five minutes; stale, absent, cross-session or malformed tokens are denied without reaching the MySQL mutation. The existing `MFA_REQUIRE_PRIVILEGED=true` policy still requires genuine session MFA. Enabling these controls does not permit customer dispatch or production release.
2. **Read-only authoritative internal scope checks.** `server/commercialIntegrity/checks.ts` uses integer-paise calculations to reconcile original quote lines, subtotal, GST estimate, unit costs, revisions, gross margin and independent review identity to held release snapshots and their disabled outbox. It reconciles a supplier-reviewed purchase request, quoted unit-cost lines, status and separate reviewer approval to a `HELD` PO snapshot with disabled dispatch. Rejects tampered line amounts, mismatched totals, stale/wrong review roles or authors, revision/quote-price mismatch, missing policy override, forged held-line scope or illegal dispatch state. These are **internal consistency checks**, not audited source-of-truth verification.
3. **Authenticated owner-only inspection API.** `GET /api/commercial-integrity/quotes/:id` and `GET /api/commercial-integrity/procurement/:id`, mounted in `server.ts`, enforce original session/role checks and the configured MFA policy. Route parameters are validated UUIDs and parent rows scoped to `created_by = current authenticated owner`. All reads occur under a transaction and the parent row lock; transaction commits without writes. The response contains only aggregate booleans/anomaly codes (no contacts, supplier tax IDs, bank accounts or credentials), always `productionReleaseAuthorized=false`, `productionReleaseBlocked=true`, `releaseDecision=HOLD`. Missing records and other actors' records return 404; a database outage fails closed with a generic 503.
4. **Adversarial unit and disposable MySQL tests.** `tests/bi-commercial-integrity.test.ts` checks false financial totals, margin substitution, collusive review identity, forged outbox state, mismatched held original lines, applicant quote-reference manipulation and unverified external-source flags. Existing `tests/crm-mysql.integration.ts` and `tests/procurement-mysql.integration.ts` exercise actual HTTP auth/CSRF/owner isolation, fresh step-up and real MySQL line-total tampering/readback, then restore the disposable fixtures.
5. **Preserved idempotent transaction semantics.** Existing server-issued draft IDs, UUID request-idempotency protection, per-row locks, internal quote revision and supplier review, exact held outbox status and all previous security/staging gates are unchanged. No schema migration is required in Phase 1BI; migration registry remains through `0035_bg_cross_source_acceptance_holds` and the main branch is not touched.

## What is NOT verified / completion blockers

- True independent **customer legal entity**, tax/GST authority, account consent, credit entitlement, multi-tenant authorization in the future customer portal, and actual signed customer quotation acceptance.
- Named, legally authorized supplier; independent manufacturer/SKU price quote, negotiated contract/expiry, chain of custody, original quote signature and provenance. Client-supplied `quoteEvidenceRef` is a reference **only**. Quoted prices may be coherent yet fraudulent.
- Actual authenticated MCA signatory identity, authorized eMudhra/CCA/RCAI issuance, current live CRL/OCSP and out-of-band witness authority custody.
- Real supplier delivery, stock ownership/GRN/warehouse reconciliation, accepted carrier AWB/e-way bill, official GST IRN, payment reconciliation, customer or vendor messaging.
- Independently audited cloud retention and negative delete/overwrite evidence; actual encrypted isolated restore measured/witnessed for RTO/RPO; independent legal/SECURITY/OPERATIONS/OWNER release approvals.
- MySQL is not independent immutable WORM; a privileged administrator may tamper with both a stored snapshot and its digest or with signed data. Readback audits catch many divergences but **cannot prove independent origin or externally immutable storage**.
