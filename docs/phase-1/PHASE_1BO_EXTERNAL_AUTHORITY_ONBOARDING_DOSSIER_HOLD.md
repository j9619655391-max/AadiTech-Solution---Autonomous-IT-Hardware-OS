## Threat model and intentionally untrusted scope

Phases 1BK–1BN provide server-recomputed quotation history commitments, candidate Ed25519 witness-chain verification, a local MySQL candidate checkpoint, two applicant-provided candidate witness observations, local revocation-conflict checks and a simulated recovery rollback. Even two valid synthetic signatures do **not** establish the identities of a real external publisher or the independence of the signers. A privileged database administrator could rewrite both quote source and BM/BN local ledgers unless an **externally authenticated and independently retained monotonic head** exists outside this repository and DB. Phase 1BO therefore introduces a *strict candidate onboarding and acceptance-dossier preflight*, but no real provider integration, provider credential onboarding, live CRL/OCSP, external WORM or production approval.

## Delivered fail-closed engineering

1. `server/commercialIntegrity/quoteAuthorityDossier.ts` implements domain-separated canonical SHA-256 over an exact, bounded `aaditech-quote-external-authority-candidate-v1` dossier, explicitly binding the quotation ID, source history digest, BM candidate receipt SHA-256, BN candidate packet SHA-256 and **BN pinned-candidate keyset fingerprint**. It requires four distinct internally named review roles (**OWNER**, **SECURITY**, **LEGAL**, **OPERATIONS**) with independent actor IDs, evidence hashes and `INTERNAL_PRECHECK_ONLY` decisions. These are applicant-provided review **claims**—not authenticated reviewer accounts, signed approvals or legal authority.
2. Provider enrollment *claims* must include provider organization ID, proposed signed agreement hash, certificate policy, chain evidence and signing-key custody attestation hash; all are explicitly `UNVERIFIED`. The preflight never accepts an assertion such as `APPROVED` or `AUTHENTICATED` as evidence of permission. Legitimate provider agreement/certificate identity cannot be manufactured from fixture hashes.
3. Proposed out-of-band publication evidence includes a claimed publisher ID, checkpoint head bound to the actual BM receipt, a claimed publication receipt, Object Lock evidence hash, observed time and future retention date. Freshness is capped at 15 minutes (allowing at most 1 minute future skew) and the claimed retention end must exceed 30 days. **No network retrieval or third-party object-lock validation occurs**: internally matching hashes and dates do not prove that a head exists outside MySQL or cannot be deleted.
4. Claimed CRL/OCSP evidence includes issuer ID, distribution-point, CRL and OCSP blob hashes, revocation epoch, monotonic `thisUpdate`/`nextUpdate` time window, and sorted unique revoked-key IDs. The epoch must not roll back behind BN; claimed responses must be fresh and current. **No issuer certificate path, actual signed CRL/OCSP, RCAI/CCA/eMudhra verification or independent status feed is evaluated.** An applicant asserting an issuer name or revocation epoch is not an authoritative source.
5. DR exercise evidence includes proposed encrypted archive and restore-transcript hashes, restored BM receipt/source, recovery sequence, timestamp, claimed RTO/RPO, separate operator/witness IDs and the **only permitted state `SIMULATED_ONLY`**. A lower recovery sequence or mismatching restored head fails closed. The preflight explicitly returns `actualEncryptedDrWitnessed=false`—a non-production MySQL rollback rehearsal cannot be called cloud DR acceptance.
6. `database/migrations/0038_bo_candidate_authority_dossiers.sql` creates FK-linked local candidate authority stream and attempt history. Physical MySQL `ENUM('NO')` columns enforce non-promotion of provider enrollment, independent head verification, revocation, DR and legal signoffs; `production_release` is `ENUM('HOLD')`. Attempts record locally consistent candidate dossiers or deny-only fork, rollback, source drift, evidence disagreement and revocation regression. **Ordinary MySQL is not WORM.**
7. `server/commercialIntegrity/quoteAuthorityCustody.ts` enforces transactional locking on the original quotation source and existing BM/BN streams, compares stored local heads, records the bounded dossier hash and chain predecessor, handles exact replay idempotently, and makes conflicting internal candidate reports **sticky QUARANTINED**. `inspectCandidateExternalAuthorityDossierOnConnection` reconstructs accepted local history, checks head/event integrity, upstream BM/BN continuity, revocation monotonicity and that forbidden trust fields were never promoted.
8. `scripts/migrate-bo-authority-dossiers.ts` enforces the full preceding 37 migration SHA-256 checksums and the existing migration mutex; it applies migration #0038 on a disposable database with repeat-safe checksum check. The write guard rejects **all production**, requires CI `NODE_ENV=test` + `CRM_TEST_DB=1` + `DB_NAME=aaditech_ci`, or separately approved isolated TLS staging with **all** `BM_QUOTE_WRITE_APPROVED=YES`, `BN_RECONCILIATION_WRITE_APPROVED=YES`, `BO_AUTHORITY_DOSSIER_WRITE_APPROVED=YES` and `STAGING_DISPOSABLE_CONFIRMED=YES`. No real production settings should ever enable these.
9. Existing **OWNER-only + privileged MFA** `GET /api/commercial-integrity/quotes/:id` now includes an *inspection-only* BO dossier state, anomaly codes and combined local-scope validity. There is **no HTTP dossier writer**, no live provider API, no private key, no customer quotation issuance, no supplier PO send, no stock, tax or finance posting. Existing role boundaries and the repository's `main` branch are unchanged.
10. `tests/bo-authority-dossier.test.ts` tests strict structural fields, invoice/issuer/provider shadow claims, false head, stale publication/revocation, revoked/invalid or wrongly enrolled candidate status, reviewer collusion, DR rollback and permanent `HOLD`. `tests/bo-authority-mysql.integration.ts` covers real disposable MySQL candidate dossier genesis and idempotent replay, concurrent fork, mismatched source, malformed claims, revoked-epoch regression, simulated DR rollback, forged local stream head and upstream BN/quote tax mutation/rollback restoration. CI applies migration #0038 **before** CRM owner integrity HTTP readback and performs BO's data-generating tests **after** existing CRM/BN suites to avoid interfering with legacy fixtures. The release candidate manifest and M01–M30 ledger include migration #0038 checksum.

## Independently sourced acceptance gates still missing (must not be self-attested)

Real external acceptance needs: (a) a signed and legally reviewed named provider agreement authorizing this exact environment, issuer organization and certificate policy; (b) externally authenticated pinned issuer/witness signing keys, documented HSM custodian and independent change approvals for rollover/revocation; (c) a provider-signed or independently witnessed **monotonically increasing published checkpoint** actually retrieved from an authorized publisher, retained beyond the application's MySQL/GitHub administrators; (d) real independently authenticated RCAI/CCA/eMudhra certificate chain and signed fresh CRL/OCSP response validation; (e) actual independent external Object Lock denied-overwrite and denied-delete test artifacts; (f) third-party witnessed **encrypted archive/DR restore** verified against independent signed checkpoint, measured RTO/RPO and recovery drill; (g) genuine MCA/legal-signatory, customer, supplier, HSN/GST, quote origin and price provenance; (h) distinct verified SECURITY, LEGAL, OWNER and OPERATIONS release signoffs.

**None of those are supplied by this phase.** A cryptographically correct candidate packet or internally consistent dossier may still be fabricated by one attacker. Until the external and legal evidence is independently authenticated and accepted, every release decision remains **HOLD**.
